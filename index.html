Abaya Design Studio

Place embroidery motifs on a real abaya cut, read their true size in inches, and print them at 1:1 on A4.

Open the studio → or the single-file version (works offline)

On your phone

  1. Tap Open the studio above.
  2. iPhone: tap the Share icon in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app, full screen.
  3. Android: Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
Your abayas are saved in this browser. After you save your first abaya, open My abayas and press ⬇ Backup file — then Save to Files or send it to yourself. Browser storage can be cleared, and the backup is what lets you move your work to another phone.

What it does

Real cuts

Each cut has its own measurements, and every piece — front, back, sleeve, flat panel — is measured on its own.

True size

Every motif shows its width and height in inches and centimetres, worked out from the abaya measurements.

Print 1:1

Motifs print at their real size. Larger than one sheet? Split across sheets with alignment marks, down and across.

Eraser & crop

Rub out the parts you do not want, trim the edges, or split a motif into independent pieces.

My abayas

Save any abaya and open it again any time, with every motif exactly where you left it.

80 motifs

Vertical bands, hanging strands, rosettes, solid sprigs, classic borders, pendants — or upload your own.

Before you print

  • Paper size A4, scale 100%.
  • Turn off “Fit to page” / “Shrink to fit”.
  • Measure the print with a ruler — the size written at the top of each sheet must match the paper.
On a phone the preview is shrunk so the whole sheet fits your screen. A note tells you by how much. Printing is not affected — it always comes out at true size.

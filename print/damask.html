Solid ornament, classic borders & pendants

Solid floral sprigs with graded stroke weight and a scroll at the base, symmetrical classic borders (damask, arabesque, Greek key, spiral meander), and pendant panels.

  • Print at A4 and 100% scale.
  • Sprigs and borders suit the front edge and sleeves — ≈ 235 mm tall.
  • Pendant panels suit a full area: shoulder, back, or the width of the edge.
  • The ornament is solid: stitch it as a filled area (satin), cut it from fabric and applique it, or use it as a stencil for fabric printing.
  • Want outline only, with no fill? See the note in `README.md` — a one-line change in the generator.

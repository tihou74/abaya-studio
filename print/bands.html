Vertical edge bands — embroidery patterns

A vertical format for the front edge of an abaya, with filled black dots, leaves hatched with lines (satin stitch), hanging strands and ornate rosettes.

  • Print at A4 and 100% scale — turn off "fit to page", or the real size on paper will change.
  • Each band is ≈ 235 mm tall. For a longer edge, print several copies and join them end to end — the pattern is designed to continue.
  • Black dots are stitched as French knots or beads.
  • Lines inside leaves are satin stitch, and show needle direction.
  • Rosettes and clusters are single motifs to place as you like.

# Abaya Design Studio — how to use it

## Opening it

There are **two files**. Pick the one that suits your device:

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `designer.html` | **Computer** — needs `motifs-data.js` beside it |
| `designer-single-file.html` | **Phone, or anywhere** — one self-contained file |

### On a computer

1. **Extract the ZIP first** — do not open the panel from inside the archive.
2. Open the `panel` folder.
3. Double-click **`designer.html`**.

It opens in your browser. **No internet and no installation needed.**

> If you see "The motif data file did not load", then `motifs-data.js` is not
> beside the page — extract the whole archive. Or just use the
> **single-file** version, which needs no other file.

### On a phone

Use **`designer-single-file.html`** — the motifs are built into it, so it
needs no companion file. It is about 1.4 MB.

**Android:** download it (or send it to yourself on WhatsApp or email), then
from the Files app tap it and open it with **Chrome**.

**iPhone / iPad:** save it to the **Files** app. Tapping it opens a preview
where the buttons do not work, so install a browser that can open local
files — **Documents by Readdle** (free) works — and open it from there.

> **Why the difference?** iOS opens local HTML in a restricted preview rather
> than a full browser.

## The five steps

### 1 · Pick the abaya cut

Choose from the dropdown. **Or upload your own cut image** — it appears
behind the drawing so you can place motifs on it, and you can lower its
opacity.

### 2 · Enter the abaya measurements

This is the most important step: **the true size of every motif is worked
out from it.**

- **Length** — shoulder to hem. Default **56 inch**. The equivalent in
  centimetres is shown beside it.
- **Width** — at the chest, garment laid flat. Default **24 inch**.

The two blue lines on the drawing show exactly what is being measured:
length down the left, width across the hem. Use **Show measurements** to
hide or show them.

> **Why is width measured at the chest and not at the hem?**
> Because the hem flares differently from cut to cut. Under the width field
> you can see the resulting hem width for the cut you picked — a royal
> flared abaya has a far wider hem than a straight one, while both keep a
> 24 inch chest.

### 3 · Choose a motif

From the library (80 motifs). Use the round chips to filter by family:
vertical bands, hanging strands, rosettes, solid sprigs, classic borders,
pendants and more.

**Tap a motif** and it is added to the abaya.

**Or upload your own motif** from the "upload your own motif" field. It goes
into the **My motifs** family, works with every tool (resizing, true-size
readout, A4 printing), and is remembered by your browser.

| Format | Recolouring |
|---|---|
| **SVG** | can be recoloured to any colour |
| **PNG / JPG** | colours are baked in and cannot be changed |

You can upload several files at once. **Delete my motifs** clears them all.

### 4 · Place it and colour it

- **Drag** it where you want.
- **Resize** it with the blue corner handle, the Size slider, or by
  **typing the height in inches**.
- **Rotate** it with the slider.
- **Repeat it downwards** (useful for abaya edges), with an adjustable gap.
- **Colour** it from the swatches or with a custom colour.
- Buttons: Flip · Centre · Duplicate · Delete.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys nudge the motif (hold Shift to move faster),
  Delete removes it.

> **The size updates live** in the "True size on the abaya" box: in inches
> and centimetres, together with **how many A4 sheets it will need** — so
> you know before printing whether it is one sheet, two, or more.

### 5 · Print the motif at true size

- **Print this motif alone (A4)** at the bottom of the properties panel, for
  the selected motif.
- **Print motifs only (A4)** at the top, for everything on the abaya.

A preview opens. A coloured badge tells you what you are about to print:

- 🟢 **Motifs only — no abaya**
- 🟡 **Abaya image — reference only, not for stitching**

Then press **Print now**, and in the print dialog choose **Save as PDF** or
print on paper.

---

## ⚠️ Most important warning — read before printing

In the print dialog:

| Setting | Correct value |
|---|---|
| Paper size | **A4** |
| Scale | **100%** |
| Fit to page / Shrink to fit | **off** ❌ |

**If "Fit to page" is left on, the printed size changes** and the motif will
no longer match the position you chose on the abaya.

**To check:** measure the print with a ruler — the size written at the top of
each sheet must match the actual size on the paper.

---

## The motif is bigger than one sheet?

It is split automatically across several sheets, **both down and across**:

- Each sheet is labelled with its position: **"sheet 3 of 9 (row 1, column 3)"**.
- Sheets **overlap by 10 mm**.
- A **✂ cut along this line** mark sits on the shared edge.
- A blue line on the neighbouring sheet reads **"▲ Lay this line over the cut
  line…"** — horizontal for rows, vertical for columns.

Lay the sheets out in rows and columns as labelled, cut and tape along the
marks, and you get the complete motif at the correct size.

> Example: a pendant panel at 15.5 × 23.5 inch needs **9 sheets**
> (3 across × 3 down). The count is shown before you print.

## Quick answers

**Is my design saved?**
Yes, automatically in the browser. Close the page and reopen it and your
design comes back. **New design** clears it and starts over.

**I want a picture of the design to send to a client.**
**Abaya image (reference)** produces a page with the abaya drawing and a
table of every motif with its size and colour. Save it as PDF.

**The lines are thin — I want them thicker for embroidery.**
The motif files in the `bands` and `damask` folders are vector (SVG), so open
one in any vector editor and change the stroke width. Quality does not
degrade however much you enlarge it.

**Can I print from my phone?**
Press **Print now**, then choose **Save as PDF** from the share sheet and
print the file later — keeping **100% scale** and **Fit to page off**.
